[h1]Shape data[/h1]

Each object's classic-mode SHP: its size in classic pixels and its frame count.

[b]Units[/b]

[code]
R2APOC width=56 height=56 frames=64
R2PRIS width=48 height=48 frames=64
[/code]

[h2]Frame layout[/h2]

R2APOC and R2PRIS use the stock tank layout: frames 0-31 are the hull and 32-63 the turret, 32 facings each, counter-clockwise from north.
