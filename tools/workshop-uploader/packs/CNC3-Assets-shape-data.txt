[h2]Frame layout[/h2]

C3MK3, C3PRED, C3MK3RAIL and C3PREDRAIL: frames 0-95 are the hull, 32 facings counter-clockwise from north with 3 tread steps each (frame = 3 x facing + step; step the treads while the tank drives), and 96-127 the turret, 32 facings.
