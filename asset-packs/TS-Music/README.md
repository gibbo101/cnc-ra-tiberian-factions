# TS-Music

Tiberian Sun and Firestorm's in-game music, for Command & Conquer Remastered Collection mods (Red Alert). One of the asset packs from [Tiberian Factions](https://github.com/gibbo101/cnc-ra-tiberian-factions).

## Contents

**Music (Data/AUDIO/MUSIC)** (26): TS_MUS_APPROACH, TS_MUS_DEFENSE, TS_MUS_DEPLOY_MACHINES, TS_MUS_DUSK_HOUR, TS_MUS_ELUSIVE, TS_MUS_FLURRY, TS_MUS_GLOOM, TS_MUS_HACKER, TS_MUS_HEROISM, TS_MUS_INFILTRATION, TS_MUS_INFRARED, TS_MUS_ION_STORM, TS_MUS_KILLING_MACHINE, TS_MUS_LINK_UP, TS_MUS_LONE_TROOP, TS_MUS_MAD_RAP, TS_MUS_MUTANTS, TS_MUS_NOD_CRUSH, TS_MUS_PHAROTEK, TS_MUS_RAIN_IN_THE_NIGHT_PART_2, TS_MUS_RED_SKY, TS_MUS_SCOUT, TS_MUS_SLAVE_TO_THE_SYSTEM, TS_MUS_TIME_BOMB, TS_MUS_VALVES, TS_MUS_WHAT_LURKS

## Using it

1. Copy the files under this pack's `Data/AUDIO/MUSIC` folder into your mod's, or enable the Workshop pack beside your mod: the game finds music in any enabled mod.
2. Name the tracks in your mod's `Data/XML/AUDIO/MUSICEVENTS.XML` playlists.
3. For the jukebox, add an entry per track to `<MusicJukeboxTracksList>` in `AUDIO_SYSTEM_CONSTANTS.XML`, with its title in your `ModText.csv`.
